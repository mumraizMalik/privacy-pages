Hisaab Kitaab – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026

Hisaab Kitaab (package com.mumraiz.hisaabkitaab) is a simple cashbook and udhaar (credit) ledger app for shopkeepers and personal use, developed by Muhammad Mumraiz Malik ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: your data stays on your phone. We do not collect, store, or share it.

1. Data stored on your device

Everything you enter in the app (cash in / cash out entries, amounts, notes, categories, party names and phone numbers, and balances) is saved only locally on your device in an on-device SQLite database. This data is never uploaded to us or to any server.

2. No account, no data collection

  • No sign-up or login is needed. We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number.
  • We do not collect personal or financial information, location, contacts, or device identifiers.
  • The app works fully offline and does not need an internet connection for its features.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties, because we never receive it.

3. No ads, analytics, or tracking

This version of Hisaab Kitaab (v1) contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking SDKs. If a future version adds ads, analytics, or in-app purchases, we will update this policy and the app's Data safety section on Google Play before that version is released.

4. PDF statements and sharing

You can create a PDF statement for a party. The PDF is generated on your device. It leaves your device only if you choose to share it using Android's share menu (for example WhatsApp or email). After that, the app you share it through handles it under its own privacy policy.

5. Permissions

The app does not request sensitive permissions such as location, contacts, camera, microphone, or SMS. Some standard Android permissions (such as internet access) may be included by the app framework, but the app does not use them to send your data anywhere.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual entries in the app (long-press an entry). To remove all app data, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover deleted data. Please keep your own backups (for example shared PDF statements) if you need them.

7. Children

The app is a general bookkeeping tool and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, email reach.mumraiz@gmail.com.
